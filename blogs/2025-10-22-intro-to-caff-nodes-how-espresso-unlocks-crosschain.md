Rollups that value composability face a tough challenge: How do you get bridges, exchanges, and other rollups to trust your state changes almost instantly if you’re using a centralized sequencer and waiting for finality on Ethereum takes 12+ minutes? The answer? Plug into a fast base layer designed for our multi-chain future that can finalize transaction data in a matter of seconds, locking in a rollup’s state and providing a secure source of truth that external parties can rely on to act in near real time.

The new benchmark, set on an internal devnet, marks a 3x improvement in speed and 5x improvement in throughput Espresso’s engineering team has reached a major milestone: 2-second finality with 5 MB blocks on our internal development network. This represents a significant leap from our previous 6-second finality, marking a 3x improvement in speed and 5x improvement in throughput (from 1 MB/s to 5 MB/s). Why It Matters Faster finality isn’t just a technical milestone.

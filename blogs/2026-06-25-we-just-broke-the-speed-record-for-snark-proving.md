Flock, co-created by Espresso Chief Scientist Benedikt Bünz, Succinct’s Ron Rothblum, and NYU’s William Wang, is the fastest SNARK prover ever built and delivers a proof system that keeps pace with Espresso’s consensus Building trustless financial infrastructure capable of settling transactions in real time requires solving two hard problems. The first is consensus: reaching agreement on what happened, quickly and with cryptographic certainty. The second is proving, cheaply and at scale, that everything that happened actually happened correctly.

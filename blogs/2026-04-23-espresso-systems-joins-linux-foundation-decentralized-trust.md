Interoperability demands coordination. That conviction sits at the heart of everything we build at Espresso Systems, and it’s also why joining Linux Foundation Decentralized Trust is a natural step for us. LF Decentralized Trust is the premier open source foundation for the collaborative development of decentralized technology, home to projects ranging from Hyperledger Fabric to Paladin.

Presto is a crosschain framework that lets users pay on one chain and trigger actions on another, without manually bridging liquidity. Users simply pay on their source chain, and the destination chain is securely convinced the payment occurred and can safely execute the transaction. Read on to learn more, or jump right into our docs for a tutorial on building with Presto.
